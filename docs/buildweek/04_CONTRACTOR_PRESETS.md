# Contractor Profile Presets

Module: `tenderfinder_presets.py`. Workbooks: `config/presets/*.xlsx`
(generated by `config/presets/generate_presets.py`).

Presets are **alternate validated keyword workbooks**. The scoring engine is
unchanged and fully data-driven; a preset is selected by pointing
`load_keywords_config` at a different workbook. No preset-specific `if`
conditions exist in the scoring/routing code.

## The three presets

| Preset ID | Version | Behaviour |
|---|---|---|
| `civil_contractor` | 1.0.0 | Byte-for-byte copy of the canonical workbook. Preserves the original Civil Contractor scoring exactly. Penalizes vertical/interior building scope. |
| `multifamily_residential` | 1.0.0 | Neutralizes the civil-oriented penalties on interior/mechanical/electrical/HVAC/suite scope and adds residential positives (apartment, townhouse, condo, wood-frame, mid-rise, …). |
| `general_contractor` | 1.0.0 | Neutralizes vertical-work penalties and adds broad commercial/institutional/residential positives. Values both civil and vertical scope. |

## Demonstrated differentiation (from the test suite)

| Record | Civil | Residential | General |
|---|---|---|---|
| Civil (watermain / sanitary / subdivision servicing) | 100 | 100 | 100 |
| Residential interior (apartment + interior + mechanical + electrical + suite) | 0 | 44 | 53 |

The Civil preset penalizes the interior record to 0; the Residential and General
presets do not, exactly as intended.

## Capabilities

- stable IDs + semantic versions, recorded in manifests (`preset_manifest_fields`);
- `identify_preset(path)` maps a workbook back to its preset;
- `apply_preset_to_copy(preset_id, dest)` copies a preset to an editable location
  and **refuses to overwrite** an existing (customized) workbook without
  `overwrite=True`, so a founder's edits are never silently clobbered;
- the active preset is shown in the GUI's contractor-profile selector.

The AGENTS.md guidance (don't retune Tybo weights/gates without sign-off) is
honoured: the Civil preset is an exact copy, and Residential/General are additive
new workbooks, not edits to the canonical scoring.
